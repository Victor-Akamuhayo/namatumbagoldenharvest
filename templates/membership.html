Why join us?

✓ Member ownership and active participation in a local cooperative
✓ Affordable agricultural and business loan opportunities

✓ Better markets, stronger trading opportunities and financial security
✓ Stronger community partnership and shared prosperity

Membership requirements

Requirements should be confirmed with the SACCO’s approved policy and membership procedures before publication. In general, members are expected to:

  • Be a farmer, trader or community member approved for membership
  • Provide valid identification and contact details
  • Agree to SACCO membership obligations and participation
  • Complete onboarding and registration procedures
