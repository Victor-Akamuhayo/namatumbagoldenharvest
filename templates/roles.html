Important access principle

A member has access only to their own information. Internal staff roles are distinct from ownership and are assigned based on responsibilities within the SACCO’s governance and management model.

  • Super Admin: full system administration and oversight
  • SACCO Management: strategic leadership and governance support
  • Finance, Loan, Membership, Agricultural and Content roles: operational responsibilities
  • Member: personal account access only
