About the SACCO

Empowering farmers and traders with unity, accountability and opportunity

Who we are

Namutumba Golden Harvest Farmers and Traders SACCO is a member-focused cooperative organization created to support the economic growth of farmers, traders and rural entrepreneurs. We believe in inclusive growth, shared prosperity and practical support for sustainability.

Our work is centered on financial inclusion, agricultural development and enterprise growth. We exist to create opportunities that help members access savings, loans, extension knowledge, farm inputs, market opportunities, and long-term resilience.

Our mission

To empower farmers and traders by providing practical financial, production and marketing support that promotes sustainable business growth and improved livelihoods.

Vision

To be a strong, trusted and innovative SACCO that enables members to achieve sustainable prosperity and resilient livelihoods.

Purpose

To promote savings culture, improve access to finance, support productive agriculture and strengthen local trade and enterprise development.

Values

Unity, transparency, accountability, service, inclusion, entrepreneurship and responsible stewardship.

Why choose us

We are strengthening the future of agriculture and trade

  • Member-owned and member-focused
  • Community-based support for rural development
  • Affordable credit and savings arrangements
  • Business and agricultural empowerment
  • Transparent, accountable service delivery
  • Practical support for productivity and trading
